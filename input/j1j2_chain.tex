\section*{Frustrated $J_1$-$J_2$ Heisenberg Chain}

\begin{equation}
H = J_1 \sum_{i} \mathbf{S}_i \cdot \mathbf{S}_{i+1}
  + J_2 \sum_{i} \mathbf{S}_i \cdot \mathbf{S}_{i+2}
\end{equation}

where $J_1 > 0$ is the nearest-neighbour antiferromagnetic coupling and
$J_2 \geq 0$ is the next-nearest-neighbour coupling (frustration parameter).
We assume periodic boundary conditions and a chain of length $L$.
Set $J_1 = 1$.

The frustration ratio $\alpha = J_2/J_1$ is the key parameter.
Explore $\alpha \in [0, 1]$.
