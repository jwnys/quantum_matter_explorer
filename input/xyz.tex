\section*{$XYZ$ model}

\begin{equation}
H = \sum_{i=1}^{L-1} \left( J_x S_i^x S_{i+1}^x + J_y S_i^y S_{i+1}^y + J_z S_i^z S_{i+1}^z \right) - h \sum_{i=1}^{L} S_i^z
\end{equation}

where $J_x, J_y, J_z$ are the nearest-neighbour couplings.
Set $J_x = 1$.

Explore the parameter space:
- $J_y/J_x \in [-2, 2]$
- $J_z/J_x \in [-2, 2]$
