\section*{Randomized Ising model}

On a 1d chain, we consider the following Hamiltonian for the Ising model with random couplings and random fields:
\begin{equation}
H = - \sum_{<i,j>} J_{ij} S^z_i S^z_j + \sum_{i} h_i S^x_i
\end{equation}
where $J_{ij}$ are random nearest-neighbour couplings and $h_i$ are random local fields.
The ranges are:
- $J_{ij} \in \mathcal{U}[0, 1]$
- $h_i \in \mathcal{U}[0, h_{\max}]$
with $\mathcal{U}[a, b]$ denoting the uniform distribution over the interval $[a, b]$.
In other words, the couplings and fields are drawn independently from uniform distributions.
The system is therefore also not translationally invariant.

Study the model for different values of $h_{\max}$ and system sizes.
